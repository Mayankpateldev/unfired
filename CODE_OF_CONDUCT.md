# Community code of conduct

People deserve dignity regardless of employment status, seniority, nationality, race, gender, disability, religion, age, or technical experience. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-organized spaces.

Be specific, kind, and constructive. Critique ideas and behavior. Respect consent, privacy, different time zones, and the limits of volunteer labour. Nobody must disclose a layoff, identity, or personal history to participate.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, spam, fabricated claims, and coordinated retaliation against people or organizations are unacceptable. Do not publish confidential material or pressure people into unpaid work or public storytelling.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock threads, warn participants, or restrict access proportionate to the behavior. Repeat or severe violations can result in a ban. Maintainers should explain decisions without disclosing private information.

To report a concern, use GitHub's private reporting tools where available; do not reproduce harmful private content in a public issue. For maintainer misconduct or when no private project channel exists, use GitHub's platform reporting route. A dedicated project contact is not configured yet. There is no guaranteed response time or confidentiality beyond the chosen platform's controls.

This is an original project policy, not a claim of affiliation with any external conduct program.
