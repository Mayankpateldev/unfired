# Outreach you can adapt

## A former colleague

Hi [name], my role recently ended and I’m exploring [role or area]. I enjoyed working with you on [public-safe example]. If you know a team that needs [specific skill/outcome], would you be comfortable introducing me? No pressure — I can send a short summary of my work.

## A hiring contact

Hi [name], I’m interested in [role] because [specific connection to their work]. I’ve worked on [truthful, shareable example], and here is [public evidence]. Would a short conversation about the team’s needs be useful?

## User research

Hi [name], I’m exploring a free tool for [task]. Would you be willing to spend 15 minutes explaining how you do it today? Please do not share confidential or personal records. I’m trying to learn whether the problem is worth solving; there is no sales pitch.

## One respectful follow-up

Hi [name], following up once on my note about [topic]. If the timing is wrong, no response needed. Thanks for considering it.

Personalize and send individually. Do not automate mass messages or imply introductions, consent, or endorsements you do not have.
