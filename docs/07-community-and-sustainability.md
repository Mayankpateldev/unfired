# Community, governance, and sustainable maintenance

Unfired is an early contributor-led project. Maintainers are the people with repository write access; that list is visible in GitHub when published. No foundation, nonprofit status, funding, or support staff is claimed.

## Decision-making

For a small change, open an issue or pull request with evidence and a clear outcome. For a major license, scope, or data-handling change, open a proposal, allow at least seven days for discussion, and record the maintainer decision and reasoning. Seven days is a project target, not a promise that volunteers will respond. Use CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md for conduct concerns.

Do not merge your own substantial code change without review when another maintainer is available. If no reviewer is available, record the limitation in the release notes. Credit documentation, translation, accessibility, testing, and design work alongside code.

## Weekly rhythm

One optional public update: shipped work, requests for help, maintenance limits, and next tasks. No individual is expected to disclose their employment or financial status. Pair sessions and office hours can be proposed; none are currently scheduled or staffed.

## Keep the core accessible

- Local tools should keep working without an account or paid API.
- State runtime, storage, and optional hosting costs. Do not advertise unlimited free hosting.
- Keep source, documentation, and export available. Contributions are MIT licensed.
- Paid consulting, migration, training, and hosting can support contributors without changing the public license.
- Donations or sponsorships require an actual configured recipient and transparent terms. No donation channel is configured in this starter.
- If a tool becomes unmaintained, mark it clearly and document export/fork options.

## Measure useful outcomes

Track merged improvements, independently reproducible demos, installation friction, resolved bugs, and opt-in feedback. A star is a bookmark, not proof of impact. Publish job outcomes or customer savings only with consent, evidence, and a clear measurement method. Collect no personal tracking data by default.

## Handling disagreement

Focus on behavior, technical evidence, and users' needs. Do not name targets for retaliation or coordinate harassment. If a thread becomes harmful, maintainers may lock it, remove content, or restrict participation. Record significant decisions without repeating private details.
