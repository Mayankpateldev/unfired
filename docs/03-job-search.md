# Find your next role without making applications your whole life

## Define the target

Write: “I help [type of team] achieve [outcome] using [skills], and can show [evidence].” Pick two role families for the next two weeks. Read a small sample of real job descriptions and note recurring requirements instead of chasing every new tool.

## Build evidence

Use [resume-evidence.md](../templates/resume-evidence.md) to turn work into truthful, public-safe stories. Each needs a problem, your action, and an observable result. Never invent a metric. If a number is confidential, describe the kind of improvement without exposing it.

Your portfolio can be one small project with a readable README, a five-minute demo, sample data, limitations, and reproducible setup. A working CSV utility with tests is stronger evidence than a collection of generated unfinished applications.

## Keep a light pipeline

Initialize `.unfired/applications.csv` with the CLI. Recommended states: `researching`, `applied`, `conversation`, `interview`, `offer`, `closed`. Use ISO dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Enter one next action and a follow-up date. The report summarizes **current states**, not historical transitions or conversion rates.

At a weekly review:

- Few replies? Revisit targeting, evidence, and introductions before increasing volume.
- Replies but few interviews? Improve your short explanation of fit.
- Interviews but no offers? Practice specific examples and ask for actionable feedback when appropriate.
- Exhausted? Reduce the cadence and ask someone to help review the plan.

These are diagnostic prompts, not guarantees or statistical conclusions from small samples.

## Use AI as an assistant

Ask for interview questions based on a public job description, critique a redacted resume, or role-play a customer conversation. Check facts and keep your own voice. Do not send private employer material, identity documents, or another person's details to a model without authorization. Never use AI to fabricate experience or assessments.

Example prompt:

> Here is a public job description and a redacted list of my real achievements. Identify evidence gaps. Ask clarifying questions rather than inventing experience. Suggest three interview stories and mark every claim that needs verification.

## Interview and offer preparation

Practice a two-minute introduction, one debugging example, one difficult collaboration, and one tradeoff you would handle differently now. Ask how the team measures results, trains staff, uses AI, and handles role changes. Review a written offer for compensation, location, working arrangements, benefits, and contractual terms with appropriate local help when needed. Keep [outreach scripts](../templates/outreach.md) specific and respectful; no mass spam.
