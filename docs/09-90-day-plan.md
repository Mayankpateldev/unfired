# A flexible 90-day plan

Adjust this to your finances, health, responsibilities, and local job market. These milestones are options, not a condition for belonging here.

| Period | Stabilize / earn | Learn / build | Small deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Check separation documents, benefits, deadlines, and support | Set up a private workspace | A written list of next actions |
| Days 4–7 | Model runway and choose income priorities | Pick one learning path | A weekly plan you can sustain |
| Week 2 | Speak to contacts and target suitable roles | Finish the first exercise | A truthful resume evidence sheet |
| Weeks 3–4 | Practice interviews or scope a small paid service | Complete a tiny original tool or docs contribution | A reproducible public-safe demo |
| Month 2 | Review job-search feedback and income needs weekly | Test with willing users; improve one workflow | A useful release with limitations |
| Month 3 | Reassess role, service, or team direction | Document handover and maintenance | A portfolio case study and next-month plan |

Every Friday ask: what moved me closer to income, stability, or a useful result? What did I learn from people rather than feeds? What should I stop? Reduce scope when the plan is costing more than it helps.

Use [weekly-plan.md](../templates/weekly-plan.md). If cash pressure rises, prioritize local support and income tasks over unpaid open-source work. You can contribute later or in small pieces.
