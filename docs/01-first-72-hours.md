# First 72 hours: stabilize before you sprint

You do not need to start a company today. A layoff is a change in employment, not a verdict on your ability. Ask someone you trust to sit with you while you make a short plan.

## Today: make the important dates visible

- [ ] Write down your final working day, access end date, benefit/insurance end dates, equipment-return date, and any response deadlines.
- [ ] Request a written breakdown of final pay, unused leave, severance, expenses, benefits, and relevant equity dates. Ask which items are estimates.
- [ ] Keep personal employment records you are entitled to retain: your contract, pay statements, separation notice, and benefit information. Use the approved HR process. Do not export shared drives, internal messages, customer files, or source code.
- [ ] Ask how to receive employment/tax records after your work account closes. Give HR a personal email through an approved channel.
- [ ] Read before signing. Ask for time and clarification. If terms, deadlines, immigration status, or compensation are unclear, consult a qualified local adviser promptly.
- [ ] Tell one trusted person what happened and what support would help: a meal, a walk, childcare, or help with paperwork.

## Next 24–48 hours: cash, benefits, and access

- [ ] Check your local unemployment/benefit process and deadlines using official sources. Eligibility and reporting rules vary; do not assume severance means you should delay checking.
- [ ] In Canada, Service Canada says to apply for EI as soon as you stop working, even without your Record of Employment; delaying more than four weeks can lose benefits. Read the [official EI overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/ei-regular-benefit.html) for current instructions and eligibility links.
- [ ] In the United States, use the [Department of Labor worker layoff resource](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/layoffs/workers) to find support and state-specific next steps. This guide does not determine eligibility.
- [ ] For other locations, find your government labour and benefits agencies. Use the [regional resource template](../templates/regional-resource.md) to contribute a sourced guide.
- [ ] List cash available now and essential monthly costs. Keep promised payments separate until confirmed. Use [the runway guide](02-runway-and-income.md).
- [ ] Confirm health coverage transitions and prescription needs with your benefits provider. Avoid assuming coverage continues after your last day.
- [ ] Move personal accounts away from a work email, where authorized. Never change or retain company credentials. Complete equipment return and ask for a receipt.

## By 72 hours: set a sustainable week

- [ ] Make a private applications tracker: `python3 tools/unfired.py init`.
- [ ] Write three public-safe achievements from your career. Use approximate or non-sensitive descriptions where needed; omit confidential metrics.
- [ ] Ask two contacts for a conversation using [outreach templates](../templates/outreach.md).
- [ ] Choose one income track and one learning track. Rest belongs on the calendar too.
- [ ] Set a short check-in with someone you trust. If distress feels persistent or unmanageable, seek qualified local support.

If your permission to work depends on employment, seek qualified immigration guidance promptly. There is no universal grace period this repository can safely calculate for you.

This is a general organizational checklist. Official instructions and qualified local advice take precedence. Reviewed 2026-10-27; see [sources](sources.md).
